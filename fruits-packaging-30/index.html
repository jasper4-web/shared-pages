FRUITS · 30 packaging renditions

Thirty different directions for the 4 oz Sana Ocean jar — every one built out as a complete piece: the full side strip (the whole wrap, flat) and a matching top, plus how it lands on the jar. Route A compliance is carried on the customer-facing face of every single one (CANNABIS-INFUSED bold and above the identity · “Fruit Chews” · net weight in both units · CA triangle at 12 mm+).

Side strip 180.34 × 43.815 mm bleed Cut 177.80 × 41.28 Top 53.34 mm bleed / 50.80 cut Jar white Lid WHITE — corrected Type Futura
Compliance flag still open — California prohibits cartoons on cannabis packaging and scrutinises child appeal, and the DCC pre-approves nothing. Every rendition tagged uses the character depends on the distributor / cannabis-attorney call going our way. The no character ones survive either way — that's why they're in the set.
