The tile wall, three ways

All three are real and clickable on the HVAC page. Tier switch works, tapping anything still opens its full story, no JavaScript — same as the board that ships today.

The problem, measured on a 390×844 phone: the 16 tiles are 950px tall, and the app doesn't appear until 2,175px down — about 2.6 screens past the hero. You scroll a spec sheet before you ever see the thing that sells.

The trap to avoid: this board exists because the page used to under-sell — it showed 5 things when the packages hold 16, so it read like one small product. Hide the 16 completely and that problem comes straight back. The scroll should die; the scale should survive.
