    55% of their setup feePaid the Friday after their first payment clears, on top of your $1,000 or your base. The whole 55% of the package setup fee, even if they split it into two payments. Taxes and pass-through costs don't count.

The fine print

Up front, not in the footnotes.

Straight, so nothing surprises you later

  1. From week 5, you're a W-2 employeeYour first four weeks are paid as a contractor, by Zelle. After that, taxes come out of every check, the normal way. Health insurance isn't included.
  2. After week 4, your base is $500 a weekEvery week, for as long as you're with us. Everything above it is commission, and it's uncapped.
  3. Your costs are yoursGas, vehicle, wardrobe. We give you the demo, the sales app, your patch and your list, and a SANO tablet once you close your first client. Until then, the demo runs on your phone.
  4. The 6% runs while you're hereYou earn it for as long as that client stays and you're with SANO. It stops if your job with SANO ends, whoever ends it and for whatever reason, or if the client leaves. It's the book you build while you're here, not a pension.
  5. The gym counts as payIt starts after your first sale, and SANO pays Life Time directly. Once you're on payroll it's a taxable perk, so it shows on your pay stub.
  6. If a client ever gets a special priceWe agree your commission on it in writing before it's offered. Never after.

Some clients will cancel. If one does, you keep every dollar we've already paid you on them. No clawback. The only exceptions: a payment sent by mistake, or commission on a sale that wasn't real.
