SANO in one minute

Not software. The people they can't afford to hire.

  • We install the systems big companies pay whole teams to run: answering, booking, follow-up, reviews, bringing customers back. Then we run them. The owner never has to learn software.
  • What they're really buying is a tech team that handles all the techie stuff they don't want to deal with.
  • Who we sell to: small and medium businesses of every kind. Most of all, the ones that live on the phone and on repeat customers.
  • Why us, not an app: most companies selling to these owners sell software the owner still has to set up and run. We set it up and run it. If they already use something like Jobber or QuickBooks, we work alongside it.
  • Lead with organization, not the phone line. The first owner we built for, a mobile mechanic, barely cared about the phone assistant. What he wanted was everything organized and under control: never running behind, never forgetting a follow-up. That's what most owners want.
The question to open with: “¿Qué se le pasó la semana pasada?” “What slipped through the cracks last week?” Let the owner name their own problem. Everything below is a way to fix it.

Don't say on day one

  • That anything is “already working” for a business. Nothing is yet: we don't have clients running on it.
  • A date for the paperwork or for texting. Say “as early as we can” and “usually 1 to 4 weeks”; the phone companies set the pace.
  • That the call answering sounds like a person on their team. It says it's an assistant.
  • Translation between the owner and an English-speaking customer: if they ask, say only «Es lo próximo que vamos a construir. Todavía no está listo.» (“It's the next thing we build. It isn't ready yet.”) Don't promise it, no date.
  • Voice notes: Jasper hasn't ruled on those yet. For an owner whose business number is her own cell, use the answer in the table above and nothing more (texts to her cell never reach the app).
  • That the WhatsApp inbox (Growth and up) answers by itself on day one. Point 4 is the owner's own one-tap send.
  • Any price from memory.
