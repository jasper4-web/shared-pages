If we cannot deliver what your approved blueprint specifies, the balance is not payable. Setup fees are non-refundable once work begins. If you prepay, you pay for twelve months and the thirteenth is free. Cancel mid-year and we refund the unused months beyond your minimum term.

Guarantee and cancellation

Your first 30 days are money-back on the monthly fee. Go-live means the day your department starts answering real customers — not the day you sign, and not the day we start building. The 30 days run from go-live, and billing starts on the same day. To take the guarantee, tell us inside that window — a call or an email is enough, there is no form and no notice period — and we refund that month's fee and end the minimum term: you owe nothing further. The one-time setup is not refundable once the work has begun, as set out under Billing above. After 30 days, plans carry an initial minimum term (three months on Starter, Growth and Scale, six on Total Transformation), after which you can cancel any time with 30 days notice. Your data is exported to you free of charge.
