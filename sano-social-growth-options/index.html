RED: never, whatever the price

  • Buying followers, likes, views or comments from any seller. The FTC rule bans buying fake indicators of influence (bot or hijacked accounts) when the buyer knew or should have known, for a commercial purpose.2 The FTC's warning letters cite penalties of up to $53,088 per violation.1 TikTok removes fake followers when it finds them.7
  • Free "booster" sites and the scripts that drive them (Zefoy and GitHub scripts such as Zefoy-Automation and TikTokBoost, which advertises "ghost views"). Fake numbers, same rule.
  • Bots and AI agents that act from our account (like, follow, comment, DM): LocoAgent (1.1k stars), meta-automation (advertises "anti-ban stealth"), instagram-ai-agent (logs in with our password), instagrapi (7.2k stars), InstaPy, GramAddict, taktik-bot, and the paid service Upleap ($19–$39/month).25 Instagram: no "accessing or collecting information in an automated way without our express permission".3 X: "Automated, bulk, or indiscriminate liking ... is prohibited", and no bulk following.56 TikTok: "We strictly prohibit automation tools, scripts, or other tricks designed to bypass our systems."7 One restriction ends the creator DM plan.
  • Keyword auto-reply bots: ReplyGuy and the open-source ReplyGuy-clone. X: an app that "auto-replies to anyone mentioning a keyword" isn't allowed.5 Drafts a person sends (row 6) are fine.
  • Extra accounts or made-up people liking and commenting on SANO's posts, by us or by an AI. Fake personas, and SANO's own rules already ban it.
  • Phone farms running many accounts (for example viral-farm). Same TikTok automation rule.7

Why the money goes to the platforms

No platform publishes a cost per follower; every figure above is a guide's estimate or one advertiser's result. So the plan spends $50 to learn our real number in two weeks, then puts the rest where it's cheapest. A platform's own ad is the paid route that is aimed (US, business owners), reported (follows per dollar) and inside every platform's rules. The YELLOW and RED tools all put at risk the one thing we can't lose: the accounts the creator DMs come from.
