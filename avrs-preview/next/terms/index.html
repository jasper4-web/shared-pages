Terms of Service and Texting Terms

AVRS Automotive, LLC · Effective [the day it goes up]

These are the terms for working with AVRS Automotive, LLC, my mobile auto repair business. I'm Anthony Simmons, the owner, and I come to you. By booking a job with me or getting service from me, you agree to them. The texting terms are further down.

How the work goes

  • I work where your car is. I need access to the car and a safe, legal place to work. If I can't do the job safely there, we'll find another time or place.
  • Some online bookings, usually farther from my area, are requests until I confirm them.
  • I tell you the price before I start. If I find something different once I'm into the job, I'll tell you and get your OK before doing more.
  • Payment is due when the job is done, unless we agree otherwise ahead of time. Parts I have to order are paid up front (below).

Parts I have to order

If your job needs parts I can't get locally and have to order, you pay for those parts up front. If you cancel after I've ordered them, I keep a 20% restocking fee.

Cancelling or moving a job

Call or text me as early as you can, so I can give the time to someone else.

Warranty

Any warranty on my work is what I tell you when I do the job. Parts may carry the maker's warranty, which comes from the maker, not from me.
