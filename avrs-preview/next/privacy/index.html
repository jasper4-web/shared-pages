Privacy Policy

AVRS Automotive, LLC · Effective [the day it goes up]

AVRS Automotive, LLC is my mobile auto repair business. I'm Anthony Simmons, the owner, and I'm the mechanic who comes to you. This page explains what I keep about you, why I keep it, who else handles it, and how to have me stop contacting you or delete it.

What I keep

  • You: your name, cell number and email.
  • Where the car is: the address, town and zip where I do the work.
  • Your car: year, make, model, VIN, plate, mileage, photos you send me (like the door sticker), what it's doing and what I did.
  • Your jobs: bookings, quotes, the date of your last oil change, and the texts and emails between us.
  • Your texting choices: whether you agreed to texts, which kind, when and where you agreed, and the wording you saw, so I can show you asked for them. This may include your device's internet (IP) address. If you text STOP, I keep that too.

How I use it

  • To book, confirm and do the work you ask for, and remind you the day before.
  • To keep you posted about your job, send you quotes and answer your questions.
  • To remind you when your oil change is due, follow up on a quote I gave you, and ask for a review after a job. These come by email, or by text if you agreed to those texts.
  • To keep a service record for your car, and for taxes and other legal records.

Texting and your mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

My texting number, [your AVRS texting number], only texts you if you agreed to texts, texted it first, or called me, got my voicemail, and heard my greeting say a text is coming (one text back), and agreeing is never required to book or to get service. How texting works, including STOP and HELP, is on my Terms page.

Who else handles it

I don't sell your information. A few outside services help me run the business, and each one gets only what it needs to do that job for me:

  • My booking and messaging platform keeps my customer list and calendar, takes online bookings, and sends my texts and emails.
  • My app host runs the phone app I work from. The app can also read a photo, like a VIN plate, to fill in the details for me.
  • Phone carriers and email services deliver the texts and emails.
  • The tech company that set up these tools for me and keeps them running sees your details only to set up, fix and run them.
  • Vehicle lookups and parts suppliers get only the car details needed, like the VIN, to identify your car and order the right parts.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect my rights in a dispute.

My website

When you visit avrsautomotive.com, it uses cookies and tags from Google (Google Analytics and Google Ads) to count visits and see how my ads are doing. Google explains how it uses that information here. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

How long I keep it

For as long as you're a customer, and as long as tax and other legal records require. If you ask me to delete your details, I delete everything except records the law makes me keep and a note of any STOP, so you're never texted again.

Your choices

  • Stop texts: reply STOP to any text from my texting number. You'll get one text confirming it, then no more texts from that number. You can also tell me in your own words, by text, call or email.
  • Stop oil-change reminder, quote follow-up and review emails: just tell me by call, text or email.
  • See, fix or delete what I have: call, text or email me (details below).

Keeping it safe

I use established services with secure connections, and I limit who can see your details. No system is perfectly secure, but I take reasonable care.

Changes

If I change this policy, I'll post the new version here with a new effective date.
