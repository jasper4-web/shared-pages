How your scheduling works

Your jobs, your lengths, and room for the drive.

  1. Your menu, at your lengths.

    Diag an hour, front or rear brakes an hour and a half, full brakes three. Customers only see start times the job fits in; unknowns book as a diag first.

  2. Room for the drive.

    Each window leaves room for the drive. We size that room from the longest drive between your home towns, which you give us in “Your setup” below. If that's 45 minutes, the windows are spaced for 45 minutes. A town farther than that calls or texts you instead. A long job takes the next start with it. We'd suggest arrival windows over exact minutes; your call.

  3. You approve at first.

    Each online booking is a request you confirm from your phone. Once you trust it, and our tests show the checks hold, simple jobs close to home can book themselves.

  4. One window a day stays yours

    for no-starts and fleet. If a car won't start, the booking form tells them to call or text you.

  5. Big trucks and far towns go through you.

    For diesels, anything 3/4-ton or bigger and far towns, the booking form tells them to call or text you. You size them and put them on your days.

  6. One calendar.

    When you book a job yourself, you add it to the same calendar, so online booking won't offer that time.

Does it route around traffic?

No live-traffic routing, and it won't pick the closest tech. Each window's room comes from your longest home-town drive.

Will jobs still run long?

Yes. A long job takes the next start with it, and your held window gives you room. “Your setup” below asks who tells the next customer.
