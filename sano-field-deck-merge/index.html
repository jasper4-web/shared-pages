The merge — three drafts

Rolodex × Command Deck, three ways. Every draft is frozen at 12:40 on a working Wednesday — three doors knocked, Rudy's demo at 2:00, Marisol going quiet — so judge it the way you'd actually meet it. Test each the same way: can you tell in 5 seconds what to do next? Then open Rudy, then find Marisol's rescue text and send it.
