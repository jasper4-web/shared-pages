Reaching 5,000 to 10,000 creators: the plan

This builds on the creator promo code feasibility report and replaces the partner offer with creator codes: the shop gets about 10% off setup, and the creator gets 55% of the setup the shop pays, once. Prices come from sanosystems.co/pricing, read today. Outside numbers link to their source. our rule = a SANO decision already on file. assumption and our math are ours. The math script is kept with SANO's working files.

10,000 is reachable in about 4 months, but not by DMs alone

  1. DMs can't carry 10,000 on their own. Our own accounts top out at about 50 creator DMs a day, sent by hand across Instagram, the Facebook Page, X and TikTok. Realistically we can staff about 30 a day, because an assistant can only get into Instagram and Facebook without sharing a password. At that pace, 10,000 by DM alone takes more than a year, and even at the full 50 it takes 33 weeks. Email has to carry most of the volume. our math
  2. How: DMs go first, to the best-fit creators. Cold email goes to the creators and social media managers who list a business email. Codes and sign-up are self-serve. We go in waves, 500 → 2,000 → 5,000 → 10,000, and new spending is added only when a wave's numbers pass its gate.
  3. How long: about 2 weeks of prep, then 5,000 by week 13 and 10,000 by week 18 (roughly March and April 2027). That pace needs an assistant and 8 new email inboxes just for creators, and every gate has to pass. If a gate misses, we keep going at the slower pace: 5,000 by week 23 and 10,000 by week 44. Brandon alone, spending $0, would take more than a year. our math
  4. Cost: wave 1 costs $0. Full speed is about $542–$737 a month: $152 in tools plus $390–$585 for an assistant. Costs start in stages, so reaching 10,000 costs about $1,900–$2,600 in total, one-time items included. Nothing is bought; each item needs your yes. Two items go against rules already on file (see the red box in section 3).
  5. What to expect: at the benchmark rates, 10,000 contacts bring about 500–1,500 replies and 250–750 creators signed up. Of those, 19–57 ever send anyone, and about 3–10 clients come in, around 6 in the middle case. At 5,000 contacts, expect about 2–5 clients. Six Starter clients bring in about $9,600 over their first 3 months, after the creators are paid. Sales come late: the first paid referral usually takes 3–6 months.
  6. Wave 1 is the real test. No study shows DMs beat email with creators, so wave 1 sends 250 of each and compares them. It also tests whether hand-picked, supported creators beat the 1.28% benchmark. If they don't, stop at wave 2 and keep the few who produce.

After the message: the machine

Sign-up page
Self-serve. Rewardful's hosted sign-up page "will generate unique promotion codes" for each creator from one Stripe coupon, and can pay one commission per customer ($49 a month: codes, one-time, sign-up page). Rewardful Starter tracks up to $7,500 a month in sales from creators, and a branded page on our own domain needs the $99 plan. Or we build our own on sanosystems.co with Stripe promotion codes, for $0. Check first that the commission can be figured on the setup fee alone.
Codes and tracking
10% off setup only, first-time customers only, with a use cap and an expiry date. Stripe supports all of these (Stripe). The booking page gets a "creator code" box, and the sales app records the source. The first source logged wins.
Creator kit
3 post ideas, a 30-second clip and captions. The disclosure line: "#ad: SANO pays me if you sign up with my code." A claims sheet: prices only from the pricing page, "web app", no promised results, never "free setup".
Nudges (most affiliates never send anyone)
Day 0: welcome and the kit. Day 7: "first post?", with an offer to make a clip together. Day 30, if they haven't posted: a fresh angle. Day 60: what's working for others. Day 90, still inactive: paused. Top creators get personal deals, as Ahrefs did.
Payout
55% of the setup the shop actually paid, once, 30 days after the payment clears and after kickoff. Payouts run monthly. W-9 before the first payout; a 1099-NEC at $2,000 or more a year.
Demos
Code leads book a 30-minute call on the booking page. Brandon's capacity: about 8 demos a week (2 a day, 4 days, in the afternoon desk block) assumption. At the benchmark rates, 10,000 contacts produce about 1 demo a week, if 1 in 4 demos closes and they spread over 6 months assumption. Even if creators do 5 times better, it's about 5. If demand outgrows the calendar: the booking page shows only open slots, and waiting leads get the "See it for my business" page and a recorded demo. California leads near our field rep get an in-person visit. A full calendar two weeks in a row is the signal to add a closer.

Legal limits, one line each

  • CAN-SPAM: "The law makes no exception for business-to-business email". Opt-outs must be honored within 10 business days, every email needs a postal address, and penalties run up to $53,088 per email (FTC). Our one-line footer covers this.
  • FTC endorsements: every creator post says it's paid, and SANO spot-checks posts (FTC).
  • Platform rules: no bots, no scraping tools on Instagram, and no repeated message bursts (Meta, X, above). A blocked account means stopping that account for the wave.
  • Texas and California: we never call or text creators or owners for this. Creators only post to their own followers; they never cold-message owners for SANO our rule.
  • Taxes: W-9 before the first payout; a 1099-NEC at $2,000 or more a year. A 2025 law set the $2,000 threshold; the IRS notice on it is in the Federal Register.
  • Freelancer sites: no off-platform contact (Upwork, Fiverr, above).
What we assumed, and what isn't known
  • Assumed: the per-account DM ceilings for X (20) and TikTok (10), that Brandon keeps up 10 DMs a day on those two, 2 minutes per message, and about 20 spare email sends a day today.
  • Assumed: half of those who reply sign up, 1 client per creator who sells, 8 demos a week of capacity, and 1 in 4 demos closing. SANO's real close rate has never been measured.
  • Weak spots: the reply rates have no study behind them. The affiliate rates come from one vendor (Rewardful), mostly about software people sign up for online. Instagram's safe DM number is our own rule, not Meta's. The X help pages and the Upwork and Fiverr terms blocked automated reading, so those quotes come from the pages as search engines show them.
  • Not known: how many true trade creators exist in the US, whether DMs or email gets more replies for SANO (wave 1 measures it), and whether Rewardful can pay on the setup fee alone.
  • Dates: about 2 weeks of prep starting after Thanksgiving puts wave 1 in mid-December. The holidays will slow it.
