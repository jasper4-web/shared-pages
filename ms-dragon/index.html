Questions you might have

If I missed one, just ask me. My number is at the bottom.

  • There are a lot of little details to my job. How do I know you'll get them right?

    We ask you a lot of questions first, in person at your shop. More than you'd expect, on purpose: how you price, what paper you use, how you like files sent. Then we build it from your answers, so it fits how you work. Nothing goes to your customers until you say it's right.

  • What if someone wants a job I don't do?

    Your website lists the jobs you do. For the ones you send somewhere else, it says so kindly, and where to go.

    Jobs you send somewhere else (and where, if you like)We'll write these down together when I come by.

  • My prices depend on a lot of things. Can a website do that?

    For simple copy jobs, yes. You give me your prices for how many, paper, color and one side or two. The order page adds them up.

  • What about tricky jobs, like booklets?

    You pick which jobs go on the order page. Everything else still comes to you. The website asks for the details and the file up front, so most of what you need is in the first email.

  • Do I have to watch my phone all day?

    No. Paid orders wait for you, like your email does now. The order page tells customers when their order will be ready, using the timing you pick.

  • What if I need a day off?

    Tap "closed today" on your phone. Your website shows you're closed, and online orders pause until the next day. No call or text needed.
