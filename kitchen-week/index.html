How to cook it

Season everything your way. Weigh on the scale, raw.

🥩 3:30 beef bowl start 3:00

  1. 3:00 Oven to 425°F. Weigh 350 g potatoes, cut ¾-inch cubes, toss with 2 tsp olive oil + salt on a sheet pan.
  2. 3:05 Potatoes in. 25–30 min, flip at 15. Sweet potatoes run a few minutes faster.
  3. 3:15 Skillet, medium-high. 8 oz beef (half a pack). Break it up, brown about 7 min. Spoon off extra fat.
  4. Add a big handful of pepper/onion + zucchini or eggplant + a spoonful of garlic. Extra collards? Throw a handful in. 4 min. Season.
  5. Salad: ½ cucumber, ½ tomato, ½ avocado, cilantro, olive oil, salt.
Beef is done at no pink (160°F). The other half of the pack: wrap it tight, bottom shelf, it's tomorrow's.

🍗 6:00 chicken plate Mon · Tue start 5:40

  1. Weigh 7 oz (200 g) raw chicken. Cut into 1-inch pieces. Rest of the pack: wrap it, bottom shelf, it's Tuesday's.
  2. Skillet, medium-high, 1 tsp oil. Chicken 8–9 min, turning a few times. Cut the thickest piece: no pink, clear juice (165°F).
  3. Push it aside. Broccoli rabe + a handful of carrots + a spoonful of garlic, 3 min.
  4. No microwave: squeeze the rice pouch + ½ the black beans into a small pan with 2 tbsp water. Stir 3–4 min until hot.
  5. Cilantro on top.
Wash the board, knife and your hands after raw chicken, before you touch the veg.

🍲 6:00 soup night Wed · Thu

  1. Pot, medium heat: 2 cups Zuppa + a big handful of collards + a handful of carrot coins.
  2. Simmer about 5 min, until the carrots are tender. The collards are cut small, so they're done by then.
  3. Want the protein back? Fry 2 duck eggs on the side, 3 min.

🥣 Skyr bowl right after the gym

  1. Whole tub into a big bowl.
  2. Chop 1 apple on top. Add ½ cup pomegranate (Mon/Wed) or 1 cup grapes (Tue/Thu). Rinse the grapes now.
  3. 28 g pumpkin seeds + 1 tbsp honey.
Weigh-in: same scale, every morning after the bathroom and before the gym. Only the weekly average matters. Goal: up ¼–½ lb a week.
