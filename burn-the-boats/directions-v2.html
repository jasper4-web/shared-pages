Two things the research changed since round one

Your rival has to be beatable, or it will quietly break you. The leaderboard research is blunt: competition only motivates when it makes you feel competent. A single 91-day gap you're permanently losing does the opposite. The fix is time-segmentation — "you might be buried all-time but crushing it this week." So in every direction below, the rival is fought in weekly rounds you can actually win, with the cumulative gap kept as the quieter background number.

An OVR shouldn't be a flat average. EA rates a player by weighting the attributes that matter for their position — a defender's tackling counts more than their finishing. Yours should work the same way: you're a founder, so Discipline and Execution carry more weight than Faith does. It makes the number honest instead of decorative, and it means gaming one easy stat won't move it.

My read

All five carry your three ingredients. They differ in which one is load-bearing — and that's the actual decision, because it sets what the page makes you feel at 6 AM.

01

The Card, with the Duel's round record underneath my pick

The Card gives you the single number you'll live by and puts your weakness in red on your own identity. The Duel gives you something the Card can't — a way to win this week, which the research says is the difference between a rival that drives you and one that grinds you down. Card on top, round record below it, burn as the contract strip. It's the tightest fusion of everything you said you liked.

02

The Silhouette strongest single image

The best pure answer to "show me where I'm lacking" — a dim leg needs no interpretation at all. If I were optimizing for the one screenshot that makes you change behavior, this is it. It's second only because it needs the detail map underneath to be a complete tool.

03

The Cockpit best fuel-gauge idea

Solves the Burn problem better than anything else here — a fuel gauge is urgent without being cruel. I'd take that one idea into whichever direction you pick, regardless of whether you choose this one. Loses because gauges inform rather than move you.

04

The Duel most energizing

The round record is the single best mechanic on this page and it belongs in the final build. As a whole architecture I think it's too loud to live in for four months — but as a component it might be the most valuable thing we've designed in either round.

05

The Equity Curve smartest, riskiest

Intellectually the best fit for you and the best rendering of a bad week I know of. I rank it last only because it makes your life look like your job, and you already told me you want to feel like you're upgrading yourself — not auditing yourself. The drawdown idea, though, deserves to survive into whatever we build.
