My read

All five drop the OVR, promote the counter and keep the vices in place. They differ in where the drama sits.

01

The Ember Line my pick

It's the only one where the metaphor and the data are the same object — your fuel gauge is literally a fuse burning toward December, with a hot tip at the boundary. You'll see that shorten every single week, and it does the emotional work without spending 200px or risking looking cheap. If it reads too quiet for you, take its ember line and put it under direction 01's burning type; those two combine cleanly.

02

Type on Fire closest to what you asked for

Large letters, real flame, embers rising through the words. If you want to be hit in the face at 6 AM this is the answer, and it's the most faithful reading of your brief. Ranked second only because burning text is the highest-risk thing here to keep looking premium for four months.

03

The Pyre most memorable, most expensive

Shows the story instead of stating it, which is a better idea than any typographic treatment. But it's 212px tall before you reach any information, and atmosphere at 80% quality is just a dark box with a boat in it. Highest ceiling, highest risk.

04

The Furnace ages best

The one still looking expensive on day 91, because it never spends anything. It's also the one that most ignores what you actually asked for — you wanted flame and this gives you heat. Right answer if you trust restraint more than impact.

05

The Number boldest, riskiest psychologically

Maximum emphasis on the counter, exactly as requested. My hesitation isn't aesthetic — it's that a 112px number counting down and nothing else is a doom clock, and you've told me the thing that kills these for you is feeling behind. Pair it with something that goes UP and it's the strongest of the five.
