Burn the Boats — The Goal Engine

The spec the app is built against. Written 2026-07-25. Run: Mon 2026-07-27 → Mon 2026-12-01.

Why this exists

The first build tracked compliance (did you tick the block?) but not purpose (what was the block for?). A review agent put it exactly right:

"The schedule card contains no content — only containers. Ten identical strings, identical on all 91

days, whose only variable is which boxes are ticked."

This document is the spine that fixes that. It is borrowed, not invented — from 4DX (lead vs lag measures, the scoreboard test) and The 12 Week Year (the weekly execution score, 85% threshold).

The two borrowed ideas

1. Lead vs lag (4DX).

  • Lag = the result. "3 SANO clients." You cannot directly control it and it reports too late.
  • Lead = the behaviour that predicts it. "12 outreach conversations this week." Predictive AND within your control.
  • Scoreboard test: "Can I tell at a glance if I am winning?" If the screen fails that test, it is decoration.

2. The weekly execution score (12 Week Year).

  • execution = tactics completed ÷ tactics due
  • 85%+ = a won week. This is the documented threshold, not a number we picked.
  • The governing principle: "You are scoring your execution, not your results."

Consequence: what actually moves the OVR

blocks completed → weekly execution score → domain stats → OVR

Nothing in that chain depends on an outcome outside his control. He can run a 100% week and still not have Anthony signed — and the app must call that a won week, because it was one.

This is also what makes the rival fair. The Millionaire Version executes at 100% every week. An 85%+ week takes the round. Winnable, weekly, and objectively defined.

Rule: a level may only ever be justified by the level above it. A weekly tactic that serves no December goal is deleted, not done. That constraint is the entire point.

The six domains

Virtues (Focus, Discipline, Execution) were removed as axes — you cannot write a December goal for "Execution", and three of the original six had no measurable input path. Domains are goal-bearing; virtues are not. Focus and discipline survive as multipliers on how cleanly a block is completed.

A block credits more than one domain. Trail walk pays BODY + MIND. Workout pays BODY. Bible pays FAITH. This was an explicit owner requirement: "certain things do more than one category."

The week

Set each Friday. Choose which December goals get attacked and commit to tactics. Each daily block is bound to one tactic — so Block 1 is never "Block 1 · SANO", it is "SANO — ship the AVRS pitch page. Builds on: Tuesday's questionnaire."

The 07:45 coffee-shop ritual writes the three priorities that become the objective lines on Blocks 1–3. Until they are written those blocks show a dashed "no objective set" — a visible hole at 08:15.

The week shape

  • Mon–Fri — full load. 4 work blocks.
  • Saturday — light. Late start, ONE work block + a "set Monday up" block. ~⅓ load. Rendered as a light day, never as a weekday with five misses.
  • Sunday — CLOSED. Full-screen cross, SABBATH. No blocks, no scoring, no HP movement. Only bible/prayer and a read-only look back at the week.

Open question for the owner: the fuel gauge currently counts 91 weekdays. If Saturday carries a work block, the honest count is 109 working days. Built as 91 + Saturdays shown separately until decided.

Scoring rules (carried over, still true)

  • XP only ever goes up. HP can go down. Progress is permanent; integrity is current-state.
  • Daily HP loss floor of −15 — no death spirals.
  • Focus runs use the Page Visibility API to genuinely detect leaving.
  • Phone pass: 2/day. Tap STEP AWAY → 2:00 pause → return in time = clean. Leaving without tapping = instant break. The deliberate tap is the whole mechanism.
  • Blocks auto-mark by the clock; he taps only to record a miss, plus one 60-second evening close-out. Fewer taps — but MORE visible day, not less. Those are different things.

Layout consequence

Measured: the hero card burned 49% of page height on a number that moves once a week, while the schedule — which changes hourly — sat below the fold. So:

  1. Status bar (~90px): OVR, fuel, today's progress. Tap → full character sheet + veil, fullscreen.
  2. The Day (the hero): day rail with live now-line · "3h 45m of deep work left" · blocks weighted by duration · NEXT UP called out · every block tappable into its objective.
  3. Focus run: fullscreen, nothing else on screen.
