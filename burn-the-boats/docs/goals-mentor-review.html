THE MENTOR'S REVIEW

You handed him twelve screens of the Goals page and asked what he thought. Here is what a hard-to-please mentor says, in the order he'd say it. I have not softened it.

FIRST, THE THING HE'D SAY BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE

"Show me the Tuesday."

Twelve screens. Every single one is a planning screen — arranging, dating, ranking, committing, reviewing, migrating. Not one screen shows the plan meeting the day.

You have drawn an elaborate apparatus for deciding what matters and zero pixels of it touching a Tuesday at 9am. That is the whole objection, and everything below is a version of it.

His follow-up would be worse: "You spent five rounds on the Bank and four on this. On a normal Tuesday, which of these screens does he open?" The honest answer is none, and that is correct — but then the Goals page has to prove its worth on Friday and at the month turn alone, and twelve screens is a lot of machinery to justify on two touches a month.

THE SEVEN OBJECTIONS

1 · These are two machines bolted to the same app

I checked this in the code rather than guessing. award() — the only thing that pays XP — is called for:


blk:  ·  boost:  ·  win:  ·  clean:  ·  close:  ·  setweek:  ·  ship:  ·  trade:  ·  ch:

Nothing pays XP for goal progress. XP comes from activity, not from goal-directed activity. So the Bank has a ladder priced in XP, the Goals page produces commitments, and the two never touch. You can max your XP and move no goal. You can move every goal and the Bank wouldn't know.

That is the congruency failure, precisely located.

The fix is one line of wiring, not a redesign. Once a commitment carries goalId, the block completion that already pays XP can be attributed. Same XP, same rules — but now the Vault's bullion is visibly made of goal work, and each goal can show what it has actually earned you. One machine.

2 · The commitment and the block are the same object, drawn twice

Screen 05 shows Call 10 owners · SANO · MON · B1 · 09:00. Today shows a schedule with b1–b4. Those are the same thing. You designed it in two places and never drew the handshake.

A mentor will ask the obvious question: "I commit that on Friday. Monday 9am arrives. What do I see?" If the answer isn't "exactly that text, with SANO on it, in the block" — then the Goals page is a document, not a system.

Fix: one object. commit is the block's content. There is no tactics string and no separate prio. The Goals page writes it; Today renders it; the timer completes it; the completion pays XP to the goal. Draw that screen. It is the missing thirteenth.

3 · You capped the wrong unit

Capacity says 14 of 20 blocks. Good. But screen 01 shows 9 live goals across 5 areas and 1 WIG.

Nine goals is not a capacity problem, it's an attention problem, and blocks don't measure attention. 4DX's number is ≤2 WIGs because focus doesn't divide. A man with 2–3 hours a day and nine live goals has a list, not a plan.

Fix: cap the goals, not just the blocks. A soft ceiling — "4 areas with live goals this month" or "≤2 goals per area" — surfaced at the month turn, never as a block. And make the WIG mean something: it gets the 9am block by default and you must actively take it away.

4 · You collect confidence and do nothing with it

Screen 07 asks for 1–5 per goal. Screen 09 recites "3 → 2 → 2." And then… nothing happens.

"If you're going to ask me a question every Friday, it had better change something. Otherwise you've built a survey."

Fix: make it drive one thing. Two consecutive drops on the same goal triggers the migration prompt early — you don't wait for the month turn to face a goal that is quietly dying. That is the entire value of the signal and it costs one condition.

5 · You now have three mechanisms for recurring behaviour

  • Habits — a goal with kind:'habit', no date
  • Standards — weekly quotas on an Area (screen 10)
  • Vices — the dishwasher and smoking, with their own gauges on Today

Three systems for "a thing I do or don't do every week." A mentor will not let that pass. "Which one do I use for walks? Why is smoking different from the gym?"

Fix: one. My call is standards on an Area — vices are standards on Personal with an inverted target (at most once a week rather than at least three times). Kill kind:'habit' entirely. One concept, one editor, one place to look.

6 · Nothing tells you what dies when the week is short

Screen 05 has SANO and Fruits both committed on Tuesday. Thursday you lose the morning. Which one survives?

Rank exists in the model and is invisible in the week. So the answer gets made by whatever you happen to open first, which is exactly the "scrambled" feeling that started this project.

Fix: the week is ordered by rank, visibly, and when you go over capacity the app names the block it thinks should go — your rank, not its opinion. A plan that doesn't survive contact with a bad Thursday isn't a plan.

7 · The month turn is the best thing here and it's buried

Screen 09 — carry / change / park / drop, with "you committed 3 blocks in 5 weeks, confidence 3 → 2 → 2" — is the strongest single screen in the deck. It is the only mechanism in the entire app whose job is removal, and removal is what a man running five businesses on 2–3 hours a day needs most.

Fix: stop treating it as a monthly interruption. It is the headline feature. It should have a name he recognises, and it should be reachable any time he feels overloaded — not only when a date passes.

WHAT HE'D TELL YOU TO KEEP

A hard mentor still tells you what not to touch:

  • Areas · dates · goals · commitments. The grain is right. Don't add a fifth object.
  • Horizon as an attribute. Correct, and it's the thing that unlocks his actual request.
  • The edit trail. Genuinely good. Full freedom plus memory beats restriction.
  • Lead as the hero number. Right — it's the only number he controls.
  • Evidence as a required field. It kills vague goals at the door.
  • Personal as an Area with standards. He confirmed this himself and it's structurally right: it never ends, so it can't be a dated goal.

YOUR TWO NOTES, ANSWERED

Personal — confirmed

An Area, with standards, no end date, never "done". Exactly as drawn on screen 10. No change needed.

The fun weekly challenge — and this one matters more than it looks

"Buy a meal for a homeless person, +10 XP."

You already have this mechanism, and the fact that I nearly built a second one is the clearest proof of the congruency problem you named. It's a boost. Look at what a boost already is in the code:


BOOSTS = [ networking +250 · golf day +150 · finances hour +90 · read tonight +60 ]
        → pays XP, never scored against you, optional

That is your idea, exactly. What's missing is that the list is hardcoded — the same disease as the goals. So:

What it isA user-authored boost. No new object.
Where it livesThe Bank — boosts already live in the deposit chute.
Where it showsOne small optional card on Today. Never on Goals.
What it pays10–30 XP. Deliberately small.
SuggestionsA rotating pool the app offers. Tap to accept, and it becomes yours.
Scored?Never. Ignoring one costs nothing, ever.

Why the XP number matters more than it seems: a strong weekday pays ~445. If a challenge paid 100+ it would become a cheaper route to the Vault than doing the work, and the whole economy would rot. At 10–30 it is flavour — a reason to smile at the app — and it cannot distort the ladder. Keep it small on purpose.

And it must not go on the Goals page. A charitable act on a Wednesday is not a goal with evidence and a horizon. Putting it there would break the one rule that keeps Goals usable: it is not a daily surface.

THE TEST HE'D LEAVE YOU WITH

"Trace one thing end to end and show me every screen it touches."

Take "call ten owners." It should be:

  1. Goals — a lead measure on a SANO goal (conversations, 12/wk)
  2. Friday — committed to Monday B1
  3. Today, Monday 09:00 — it is the block, with SANO on it
  4. The timer — completes it
  5. XP — paid, and attributed to SANO
  6. The Bank — that XP is bullion on the shelf
  7. Friday — closed out, confidence tapped
  8. Month turn — carried, changed, parked or dropped

Steps 3, 5 and 6 do not exist today. That is the whole gap, and it is three connections rather than a new design. Build those and the project becomes one thing instead of four tabs that happen to share a colour scheme.

WHAT I'D DO NEXT, IN ORDER

  1. Draw the thirteenth screen — Today on Monday at 09:00, with the commitment in the block. If it doesn't look inevitable, the model is wrong and better to know now.
  2. Wire XP to goals — attribute the block award. One machine.
  3. Make boosts user-authored + a suggestion pool. Small XP. This is the fun you asked for and it's a day's work.
  4. Collapse habits / standards / vices into one.
  5. Cap goals, not just blocks.
  6. Give confidence one job.
  7. Then, and only then, build the rest of the plan.
