The actual brief: congruency

Round 1's six directions each had their own visual language. He has now picked four parts from four different languages. The job is to make them look and feel like one designed object — not a collage of the best bits.

That means deciding, and holding, one answer for each of:

  • One type scale across the big visual, the challenge modules and the graph
  • One shape language — do the modules echo the big visual's geometry, or deliberately contrast?
  • One colour logic — what earns warm, what stays cold, what red is reserved for (and red must never become a wall; see law #1 in the crew context)
  • One rhythm down the screen — the eye should travel big-visual → challenges → history in a way that feels composed, not stacked
