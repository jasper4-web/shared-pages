The tab is currently called Push. He wants it to be Goals.

What the page must hold

  • A goal per life category — the six domains: THE WORK · CAPITAL · BODY · FAITH · MIND · PEOPLE
  • December, plus intermediate milestones (he said 3-month / 2-month / 1-month; treat the exact number as a design variable, and try more than one answer across your options)
  • Weekly goals he writes each week, visibly connected to the daily blocks that already exist
  • Editing and restructuring — this is the requirement he cared most about. Show the interaction for changing a milestone, not just the read-only view. A design that only renders a finished tree has not answered the brief.
  • Some honest view of a goal with nothing scheduled against it

The design problem

Six categories × several horizons × weekly commitments is a lot of nested information on a 390px screen. The failure mode is an org chart nobody opens. The directions worth exploring are genuinely different answers to what is primary:

  • Time-first — horizons are the top level, categories nested inside
  • Category-first — six cards, each drilling into its own ladder
  • One-goal-at-a-time — a focus view, everything else collapsed (closest to 4DX's ≤2-WIG discipline)
  • The path — a single vertical spine from today to December with milestones as stations
  • This-week-first — the week is the page, horizons are context above it
  • A board — columns by horizon, cards move between them as things shift

Take at least four in clearly different directions. If two of yours could be described by the same sentence, replace one.
