What that means

KEEP and build on: The Spine · The Friday table · Six Doors. DROP: The One and The Board. He likes them as objects; they are not practical. Do not carry them forward and do not spend effort defending them.

The real ask is a single zoomable system, not three pages with a switcher. He described moving between altitudes — out to see everything, in to a specific area, in again to this Friday. A tab bar across three views is the lazy reading of this and is explicitly what he pre-empted with "not getting lazy with it."

Think about what the zoom levels actually are and defend your answer:

  • Out — the whole run. Six domains, December, where everything stands at once. (Six Doors lives here.)
  • Mid — one goal's ladder over time: December down through its milestones. (The Spine lives here.)
  • In — this week. What is committed, against which goal, in which block. (The Friday table lives here.)

Questions you must answer with the design, not with prose:

  • What is the gesture? Tap to zoom in, and what gets you back out? Pinch? A persistent breadcrumb? He is one-handed on a phone at 6 AM — whatever it is has to work with a thumb.
  • Does zooming preserve context? Zooming into BODY's ladder should not lose that he was looking at December. Zooming into Friday should not lose which goal he came from.
  • Is there continuity of object? The most convincing version of zoom is one where the same element grows into the next level rather than the screen being replaced. GSAP Flip is vendored at lib/gsap/ if a real morph would sell it.
  • Where does he land by default when he opens the tab on a Tuesday versus a Friday? The right answer is probably not the same.
