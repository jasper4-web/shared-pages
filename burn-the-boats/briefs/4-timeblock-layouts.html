The region you own

Everything between the bottom of the locked glance/graph card and the top of the schedule list. In the current app that is, in order:

  1. The day rail — a horizontal bar spanning 06:00→21:50 with a coloured segment per block and a live gold now-line
  2. "3h 15m OF DEEP WORK LEFT" with the weekday on the right
  3. The push strip — SANO 1/3 · FUNDED 0/3 · KITCHEN 0/1, plus a red line when Anthony's pitch is stale (assume this stays; another agent may move its contents but the slot is yours to place)
  4. The make-up-owed banner (only when a penance is unpaid)
  5. NEXT UP — the current or next block, its objective, and the focus-run button
  6. ALWAYS COUNTS — the small-wins chips with weekly quota pips and a running tally

Read index.html for exactly how these behave today before redesigning them.

The real problem to solve

Six competing elements, all shouting equally, stacked vertically. Ask what each is for:

  • The rail answers where am I in the day
  • The time-left answers how much working time is left
  • NEXT UP answers what do I do right now — this is the most important element on the screen and nothing may ever push it below the fold
  • Small wins answer what can I earn even if today went wrong — he called it "in the way", but it is structurally load-bearing (it is the only thing earnable on a blown day and it feeds five of the app's seven weekly quotas). Do not solve "in the way" by hiding it. Solve it by giving it a better home.

Job

Build renditions/rend-timeblock.html: 10+ layouts, each a real rendered phone mock-up at 390px, each with a short honest note on what it optimises for and what it costs.

They must be structurally different. Merging elements, changing what is primary, collapsing things behind taps, horizontal vs vertical, combining the rail and time-left into one object, making NEXT UP the hero and demoting everything else, putting wins on the rail itself, a two-column split — go wide. If two of your ten could be described by the same sentence, replace one.

Measure and report the pixel height of each. Vertical space is the scarce resource here; the header and graph card already cost ~206px before this region starts.

Done when

  • 10+ layouts, all rendered, all visibly different in structure
  • Each labelled with its height in px and a one-line trade-off
  • Lint clean, screenshotted at 390×844, and you have looked at them
  • Hand-off ranks your top three and says plainly which of the ten were weakest and why you kept them
