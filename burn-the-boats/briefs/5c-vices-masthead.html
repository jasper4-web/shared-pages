AGENT 5 — ROUND 3: THE MASTHEAD IS CHOSEN. MAKE IT REAL.

Turf: renditions/rend-vices-v3.html (new file — leave v2 intact)

The verdict

"Personally I like the Masthead the most, so let's go that direction. But let the agent know

if it wants to tweak a few things once it fully thinks about **how the page actually will look and

function — it is allowed to do some few minor tweaks**, as long as it makes sure that it

functions, looks and renders exactly how it's supposed to."

The exploration is over. The Masthead ships. This round is not about finding a better idea — it is about making this one genuinely work.

Your licence, and its limit

You may make minor tweaks to the Masthead — proportion, spacing, order, a component swapped for one that behaves better — if and only if the tweak is driven by how the screen actually functions once you think it all the way through. Aesthetic restlessness is not a reason. If you change something, say what and why in your hand-off.

What you may not do: replace the Masthead with a different composition, reopen the four kept parts, or introduce a new visual language. He chose this. Honour it.

The job: from a picture to a working screen

Round 2 was a composition. A composition is not a screen. Build the Masthead out until every state a real user hits in 91 days is designed and rendered:

1 · Both vices, fully. Render the complete Masthead for the dishwasher and for smoking, side by side. Their rules genuinely differ and the composition has to survive both:

  • The dishwasher — his codename, never speculate, never rename. Kept to roughly once a week, streak counted in weeks at or under threshold.
  • Smoking — nothing before 12:00. Daily clean streak. The 24h→32h escalating bonus.

2 · Every state. Design and show each:

  • Day one — zero streak, no history, nothing earned. This must not look broken or accusatory.
  • Healthy — a long streak running, gate holding.
  • Gate live — before noon, counting down, nothing logged yet.
  • Gate broken today — he smoked at 09:30. Honest, not punishing.
  • A slip, and the make-up owed — the escalating penance (Bible 15 min + 6 passages + photo proof → 90-min workout on top → final warning). Framed as mercy with a price. The streak is never wiped.
  • A challenge live — accepted, counting down.
  • A challenge missed — and this must cost nothing. Show what that looks like without shame.

3 · Real interaction. What is tappable, what happens, what the return path is. The screen is reached from the locked gauge strip — show how he gets in and out. Every touch target ≥44px.

4 · The four kept parts, still all present. The big visual at scale · the top visual · the hold-until challenge modules · the rudimentary history graph. Keep that graph plain — its charm is restraint and polishing it would lose exactly what he liked.

5 · Motion. The Masthead has a top visual that should reward being opened. One considered transition, not five. GSAP is vendored at lib/gsap/.

Render it, do not describe it

Every state above must be an actual rendered mock-up in the page, labelled. A written list of states is a failed deliverable. Twelve to sixteen frames is the likely shape of this.

Done when

  • Both vices rendered in full Masthead form
  • All seven states above rendered and labelled
  • Interaction, touch targets and the in/out path are explicit
  • One considered motion moment, animating
  • Lint clean, no horizontal overflow at 390px, screenshotted at 390×844, and you have looked
  • Hand-off: every tweak you made and why · what you were tempted to change and deliberately did not · anything that will be awkward when the GM integrates this into index.html
