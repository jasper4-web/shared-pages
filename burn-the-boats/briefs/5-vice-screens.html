AGENT 4 — THE VICE SCREENS

Mission: tapping a vice currently opens a flat sheet and dead-ends. Make it a destination — the streak shown grandly, its own dial brought inside, reasons to keep going, and optional challenges that make each vice its own little game.

Turf: renditions/rend-vices.html Depends on: nothing. Start immediately.

What he actually said

"Whenever I tap the dishwasher and smoke — these are my two vices — I want the screen to pop up to

show my streak a little bit more grand. You could even just bring **that same image that you

already have** but bring it next to where it has dishwasher and next to smoke. And then maybe there

can be more stuff, like reasons why I should do this. It just feels a little bit dead end

right now."

"There should be things like — if I go to the smoking one, remember it's only till noon — I can go

there and it's like 'wait until 2 PM today and claim an extra however many XP'. Just little

things to see if you can go and do even more. And if you go into the dishwasher, **'don't unload the

dishwasher this week and claim however many XP' — just to make those a little bit more fun and

interactive inside of their specific spheres**."

The two vices — read this carefully

"The dishwasher" is a deliberate codename he chose for a private vice. Never speculate about what it is. Never rename it. Never write copy that implies you know. Its rule: keep it to about once a week, with a streak counted in weeks at or under the threshold.

Smoking (marijuana). Its rule: nothing before 12:00. It has a daily clean streak, and a standing bonus for a full 24 hours clean which escalates to ~32h once he clears it a few times.

Both currently appear as dials in the locked gauge strip. Read renderGauges() and openVice() in index.html for exactly what exists.

The slip mechanic is his own and must not be weakened: a slip never wipes the streak. He owes an escalating make-up — 1st: Bible 15 min + 6 passages + photo proof · 2nd: a 90-minute workout on top · 3rd: final warning. Show this on the screen as mercy with a price, never as punishment.

Job

Build renditions/rend-vices.html with 5–6 genuinely different treatments of a full vice screen, rendered as real phone mock-ups. Do both vices in at least your top two so the difference in rules is visible.

Each must contain, arranged however your direction argues for:

  • The streak, grandly. This is the emotional centre. Weeks for the dishwasher, days for smoking.
  • Its dial brought inside, at a real size — he explicitly asked for the visual to come with it.
  • Reasons why. Copy that reconnects the vice to who he is trying to become by December. His own driving line is "Burn the boats. What is the millionaire version of yourself doing right now?" He is a Christian; scripture is welcome here if it earns its place. Do not be preachy and do not moralise about the vices themselves.
  • Challenges — the mechanic he asked for. Optional, time-boxed, XP-paying:
  • smoking: "hold until 2 PM today → +X XP", "24 hours clean → +X", escalating to 32h
  • dishwasher: "go the whole week without → +X XP" Design the shape of this system: how one is offered, accepted, tracked live, claimed, and what happens when he doesn't make it. Failing an optional challenge must cost nothing — law #1. Propose real XP numbers; a clean focus run pays 65 and a small win pays 15–30, so scale accordingly.
  • History — some honest view of how it has actually gone. Never a wall of red.

Done when

  • 5–6 distinct full-screen treatments, rendered, at least two showing both vices
  • The challenge system is designed as a mechanic, not just drawn as a button
  • Lint clean, screenshotted at 390×844, and you have looked at them
  • Hand-off ranks them, proposes final XP values, and flags anything that risks feeling like nagging
