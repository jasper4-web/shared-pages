AGENT 5 — THE BANK, PURELY AESTHETIC

Mission: the Bank tab holds the right things and shows them plainly. Make it beautiful.

Turf: renditions/rend-bank.html Depends on: nothing. Start immediately.

What he actually said

"With the bank, I think the best way to do this is really to have an agent look at it from a **pure

aesthetics way** and think: OK, how do we show all of the things that we're trying to show here in a

much more aesthetic way — and then give me another 5 to 10 renditions to look at. Again,

obviously not the first ones."

He likes what the Bank does. This is not a rethink of the mechanics. Keep every element; change how it looks and how it is arranged.

What the Bank holds today

Read renderBank() in index.html. It carries:

  1. XP balance — the earned currency
  2. Privileges — a second currency, earned not bought: held tokens worth "cut a block in half" or "make this block yours". Earned by an 85%+ week or by logging a boost. He was emphatic that rewards should be schedule privileges, not points — so these deserve to feel like objects, not a number in a list.
  3. Boosts — occasional real-world events that pay XP and a privilege: networking event, golf day, read tonight, finances hour. Never scored against him.
  4. Rewards — real-world things he buys with XP, each carrying a condition he wrote himself ("cooked + laundry every night this week → restaurant Saturday"). Currently: restaurant date, golf day, day off, a purchase — plus any he adds. Locked ones can show "~N days away" at his current earn rate.

The bar

This is the tab that is supposed to be fun. It is where he goes to feel that the work bought something. Every other screen in this app is disciplined and austere — the Bank is the one place that is allowed warmth, weight and reward.

Things worth exploring: privileges as physical tokens rather than a count · rewards as cards you can almost pick up · a claim that feels like a ceremony rather than a toast · the XP balance as something with mass · earned vs locked expressed by material rather than opacity · vaults, coins, stamps, tickets, wax seals — anything that makes a number feel like a thing.

Restraint still applies: dark, premium, house palette. Warm gold is the earned currency. It should look expensive, not gamified-cheap.

Job

Build renditions/rend-bank.html: 5–10 genuinely different aesthetic directions for the full Bank tab, rendered as real phone mock-ups at 390px. All four element types must be present and legible in every direction — this is not an excuse to drop things for prettiness.

Include at least one direction for the claim moment itself (what he sees the instant he spends 2,000 XP on a day off). He called this the emotional peak of the system and it is currently a grey 2.4-second toast.

Done when

  • 5–10 distinct directions, rendered, all carrying XP + privileges + boosts + rewards
  • At least one treatment of the claim ceremony
  • Lint clean, screenshotted at 390×844, and you have looked at them
  • Hand-off ranks them and says which single detail from each of the top three you would combine
