AGENT 2 — THE HEADER: VERSES + THE BURN

Mission: the furnace header stops being one fixed line. Most of the time it says THE BOATS HAVE BEEN BURNED; the rest of the time that line burns away and a Bible verse is revealed underneath.

Turf: data/verses.js · renditions/rend-header.html Depends on: nothing. Start immediately.

What he actually said

"The boats have been burned will always stay the main thing on there — that's why I said it will

be on there more than half of the time. But the 35% of the time, the boats have been burned can

do something cool like look like it burns up, or it can just transition away, and a good Bible

verse about manhood, discipline, something that has to do with the feel of this whole page should

pop up. But they are all Christian Bible verses."

Read that precisely:

  • The boats line is the identity. It is dominant — roughly 65% of the time.
  • The other ~35% is a transition, not a replacement: the line burns/dissolves away and a verse appears. It should feel like the fire consumed the words.
  • Christian Bible verses only. Not general motivational quotes, not philosophy, not Stoics.
  • Themes: manhood, discipline, endurance, courage, work, self-control, finishing.

Job

Part A — curate the verses. Build data/verses.js exporting a plain array. Target ~130 verses so it never repeats across the run (Jul 27 → Dec 31, all days). Each entry:


{ ref:'1 Corinthians 16:13', text:'Be watchful, stand firm in the faith, act like men, be strong.', theme:'manhood' }

Requirements:

  • Verify every reference and its wording. A misquoted verse in a Christian man's daily app is a serious failure. Use a public translation consistently (ESV or KJV — pick one, say which, be consistent) and check each one.
  • Themes across: manhood · discipline/self-control · endurance & finishing · courage · work & diligence · integrity · rising early / time · strength through weakness.
  • Keep them short enough to render large — most under ~140 characters. Long passages die at 22px.
  • Selection must be deliberate: no filler, no near-duplicates, no verses that only tangentially fit.
  • Include a dayFor(dateISO) helper giving a stable verse per date (same date always yields the same verse — no randomness that changes on refresh).

Part B — design the transition. Build renditions/rend-header.html showing 4–6 genuinely different treatments of the boats-line → verse transition, all animating live, all inside a phone frame with the real furnace header styling copied from index.html.

Directions worth exploring (do not stop at these): the letters burning upward and dissolving into embers with the verse rising through · a heat-haze wipe · the plate cooling to reveal engraving underneath · charring from the baseline up · a hard cut on a flare. Use lib/tsparticles/ for embers rather than hand-rolling.

Also decide and show: how does he get the verse if he misses the transition? A tap to toggle is the obvious answer — but a verse he can never summon deliberately is a verse he'll miss most days.

Done when

  • data/verses.js has ~130 verified verses with references, themes, and a stable dayFor()
  • You state which translation you used and confirm you checked the wording of each
  • rend-header.html shows 4–6 distinct transitions, animating, at 390×844
  • Lint clean, screenshotted, and you have looked at the screenshots
  • Hand-off names your recommended transition and why the others lost
