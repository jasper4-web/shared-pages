Round 2 worked: ten genuinely different apps, protocol followed. Two survived. This round finishes them. No new directions.

What he is actually telling you

The Line succeeded on the thing that matters — at a glance he knows what is done, where he is, and how the day will go, with today emphasised over the abstract shape of the week. That is the job of this region and The Line does it. It is underbuilt, not wrong.

The Face has the better idea and the worse execution. "Seeing the whole day in one" is the concept he responded to. He could not judge it because it was sloppy. Sloppy is a craft failure, not a concept failure — do not abandon the idea, execute it properly and let him judge it fairly this time.

Job

Deliver three things in one file:

1 · THE LINE, fully built

Take it from a good sketch to a complete, shippable region. That means every state rendered:

  • 06:05, day barely begun — almost nothing done, and it must not look empty or accusatory
  • Mid-morning, inside a block — the live state, which is how he sees it most often
  • 13:00, half done, one missed
  • 21:30, day nearly over, most complete
  • A day gone wrong — several misses. This is the state law #1 governs: never a wall of failure.
  • Off-script day — the day marked as not counting
  • Saturday — the light shape (one work block), which is a genuinely different day
  • A make-up owed — the penance banner present

And it must carry, or consciously relocate with a note: the rail · deep-work-left · the push strip · the make-up banner · NEXT UP, never below the fold · and ALWAYS COUNTS. Do not solve "always counts is in the way" by hiding it — it is the only thing earnable on a bad day and feeds five of seven weekly quotas.

2 · THE FACE, executed properly

Same concept — the whole day as one object — built with real craft this time. Be specific about what was sloppy and fix it: alignment, label collisions, legibility at 390px, what happens when a block is short, whether the current moment is unmistakable, whether it is readable at a glance in one second. Render the same eight states as The Line. If, having built it properly, the concept still does not hold up at phone size, say so honestly rather than shipping something that only looks fine in a screenshot.

3 · TWO OR THREE FUSIONS

He said "take those into consideration so we can bring them together." The Line's clarity about now-and-next, with The Face's whole-day-in-one. Genuinely different attempts at the seam — not one idea shown three times. For each, state which parent it favours and what it inherits from the other.

The process instruction — new, and it applies from here on

"Don't just come up with the first 10 ideas that you go with. I want you to **think of ideas, and

then scrutinize those ideas**, then only render the ones that survive — that way we're scrutinizing

the ideas before they even get to the gate."

So, before you render anything: generate at least 20 candidate approaches, write them down in the page, and kill most of them in writing. Include that cull in your output as a short visible section — the ideas considered, and one line on why each died. He wants to see the scrutiny, not just the survivors.

Done when

  • The Line and The Face each rendered across all eight states
  • 2–3 fusions, each stating its parentage
  • A visible cull section: ≥20 candidates considered, most killed, with reasons
  • Sloppiness in The Face named explicitly and fixed
  • Lint clean, no horizontal overflow at 390px, screenshotted at 390×844, and you have looked
  • Hand-off: your pick, and the strongest argument against it
