BURN THE BOATS · THE TODAY SURFACE · 2026-07-29

The day and the block you're in, as one thing.

Five ways to lay it out. Nothing structural changes — same blocks, same times, same economy, same taps. This is only how the day is drawn on the main page.

“I want us to have the one block that we're on and the whole day kind of all in one… the block that you're on right now is just a bit larger, but you can also partly see the other parts of the day.” — his brief, verbatim
WHAT'S THERE NOW
Two things with a gap between them: the NOW card (with the seam — one row above, one below) and the whole day, which ships closed behind “See the whole day”. The seam was round 4's partial answer to this same ask. This is the full one.
THE REAL DENSITY
A weekday is 14 rows, 06:00 to 21:50. Every drawing below uses the real day at 1:45p, mid Block 3 — so the problem is honest. Any layout that only looks good with six rows is a lie.
FIXED, NOT NEGOTIABLE
Deep blocks stay sacred and undeletable · no military time · the domain is a 3px left rule, never an outline · one lit object per screen · never a wall of failure — a passed block is faint, never red · 11px text floor, 44px tap floor, no sideways scroll at 390px.
THE TEST EACH MUST PASS
At 1:45p, can he see what he's in, what's next, and the shape of the rest — without tapping anything and without the page growing? And can he still reach a past row to correct it?
N1

One list. The row you're in swells.

There is no NOW card. There is one day, from wake to Bible, and the row you're standing in is simply bigger — it grows the objective, the button, the goal line. Everything else stays a row.

The most literal merge: the two components become one because the second one is deleted. Nothing is duplicated, because there is only one place a block can be drawn.

The day bends around now.

The one honest cost, stated: a compressed row is 11px text in a ~26px band, which is under the 44px tap floor. THE DOMAIN COLOURS GO QUIET·
A 5px dot carries a domain far worse than a 3px rule down the whole row. Six domains that were instantly readable become six dots you have to look at. That is a real loss, and it is the reason this is not the recommendation.
Also: the rail is the planner's idiom. Round 4's verdict on the Goals tab was “Today, Bank and Record are one app; Goals is a second app pasted into it.” This risks running that sentence backwards.

N5

Behind you · now · ahead. Three bands, one card.

The day stops being a list. What's done collapses to one band with a pip per block, the live block is the body, and what's left collapses to another band. Two taps reach any row; nothing scrolls.

The conservative end of the range — it keeps the most screen for the block you're in, and the day is present as shape rather than as text.

“PARTLY SEE THE OTHER PARTS”?
He didn't ask for the day to be summarised — he asked to see it. A pip is a count, not a part of his day. This option answers the “one component” half of the brief and quietly drops the other half, which is why it's drawn last and not recommended.
KEPT FOR ONE REASON·
It is the only option that would still work if the day ever got much longer — 25 rows, an away-day, appointments. Worth remembering when AWAY mode lands.
Shown honestly as the weakest fit for this brief, and kept because it's the strongest fit for a day twice this size.

THE CAROUSELBlocks as cards you swipe between. He already ruled on this: “I still don't even fully love the deck idea.” A swipe also hides the day behind a gesture, which is the opposite of the brief.
THE CLOCK / DIALThe day bent into a circle around a 24h face. Killed in round 2 and it hasn't got better — 14 blocks of wildly uneven length are unreadable on a dial at 390px, and it can't hold a button.
TWO COLUMNSDay on the left, live block on the right. Dies at 390px, which is the only width that matters. It's a desktop layout wearing a phone.
THE ACCORDIONEvery block collapsed; tap one to open it. Makes “which one am I in” a thing he has to do. The app already knows the time — it should never ask him to find himself.
A MINI-DAY INSIDE THE CARDThe live card carries a little strip of the whole day inside it. That is the exact duplication he's complaining about, moved one level down. Two truths, smaller.
HIDING THE PAST ENTIRELYDrop finished blocks off the page. The day loses its shape, and a mis-recorded morning becomes unreachable — the correction path needs that row to exist. Faint, yes. Gone, no.
PROGRESS BARS PER BLOCKA filling bar on the live row. Tried in spirit already: it turns the day into a scoreboard, and the focus run screen is where a countdown belongs.
MY RECOMMENDATION

N3 · The day bends around now — with N1's list as its skeleton.

N3 is the only one that is literally your sentence: bigger where you are, smaller but still visible everywhere else. It's also the only one that keeps working at 8:40p without a special case — the ramp re-centres itself, so the block you're in never drifts down the page.

It's one list, so there is no second place a block can be drawn — which is the actual complaint underneath the brief. And it's the shortest page of the five, which continues the direction Today has been going all along (2518px → 1481px → ~1240px).

The one thing to decide with me: whether a far row should expand-then-tap (drawn), or stay a real 44px target at 11px text and make the page taller. I'd take the expand — but it's your thumb.

Second choice: N2, if when you see it you want the block genuinely nailed to the top. Not N4 — it costs the domain colours, and not N5 — it summarises the day instead of showing it.
