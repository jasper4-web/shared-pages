The Thread

The day is one written record you keep adding to, and the block you're in is the only lit line in it.

What it is. One document, top to bottom, that opens at right now. Everything already lived is his own handwriting — no cards, no ticks. Everything ahead is one dim line. Exactly one thing on screen has a border: the block he is in. Tap any line to write into it.

What it gives up. The day at a single glance — you scroll for the shape, and one sentence at the top carries the count. It is a record, not a schedule.

01
Notes on a block. Writing is the architecture — the objective and notes are the body of a block, and its earlier runs are the same thread, scrolled back.
02
Merge NOW and the day. Nothing to merge: NOW is a position in the day, not a second component.
03
Wins as by-product. Lines at the foot of the page where the day's residue collects — offered once, in passing, when a block ends.

Two rules held. No checkbox anywhere, so nothing can sit unticked accusing him. No x-of-y counter anywhere: the count only goes up.
