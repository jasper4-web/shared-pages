The controls, five ways.

Same New-goal sheet, five different control languages. Whatever you pick gets applied everywhere — area chips, dates, durations, the planner, the Bank editors. Tap around; everything here is fake but pressable.

D1Soft Tokens

Kill every outline. Chips become filled pills in the body font — selection is a tinted fill, not a box. The friendliest of the five; closest to what modern iOS apps do.

What changes app-wide: every mono-caps chip becomes a sentence-case pill; the craft floor stays (46px+). Risk: loses a little of the app's “instrument panel” character.

D2Editorial

No boxes at all. Choices are words; the chosen word is inked and underlined in its color. Inputs are ruled lines, not wells. The quietest, most premium-print take.

What changes app-wide: enormous calm — but tappability relies on spacing alone, and with many options (seven areas) an underline row wraps less gracefully than pills.

D3The Rail

Options live INSIDE one grooved container and the chosen one is a lit cell — an instrument, not scattered buttons. Keeps the mono voice. Closest to the app's existing soul.

D4Beam

Keep the outline language but make selection GLOW — a gradient beam border and a soft halo, like the SANO site's beam cards. The most “premium tech” of the five.

What changes app-wide: unselected stays as-is, chosen things become jewelry. The glow must stay rationed — only the SELECTED thing beams, or the page turns into a fairground.

D5Tactile

Buttons with physical depth — a subtle top light, a hard 2px drop, a press you can feel. Every option carries its little domain light. The most “buttons feel like buttons”.

What changes app-wide: everything pressable gains weight and a real :active dip. Heaviest look of the five — on dense screens (20 week slots) it can get loud.

My recommendation

D1 Soft Tokens as the base, with D4's beam reserved for the single selected item on gold decisions. D1 fixes exactly what you named — the boxy, mono-caps sameness — while keeping the dark instrument feel; the beam then gives chosen things one touch of jewelry without lighting the whole page. D2 is the most beautiful but fights big option rows; D3 is the safest but changes the least; D5 is charming and a bit loud at density. Say a direction (or a mix, like “D1 with D3's rails for durations”) and it goes app-wide.
