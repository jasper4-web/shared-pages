Every past slot in THE WEEK still reads "Open — tap to commit it." Taking that offer on a Friday, for a Monday you already worked, creates a commitment that is born complete — it counts as a finished block toward that goal and lifts the week's percentage. The agent reported this happens on sync; it's actually stamped at creation, by setCommit itself.

Why it matters: the number stops being true, and it's you who'd be quietly inflating it. That breaks the forward-only rule — the past is supposed to be settled.

A completed block freezes what it credited. A missed block never got that stamp, so blockDomains() re-derives it live from the goal's current area. Move a goal today and every past miss moves with it.

This one is sneaky. It took me three attempts to reproduce: with two days of history the curve rounds it away entirely. It only becomes visible once history accumulates — so it would have surfaced in November, not now. The comment above that function says the author fixed exactly this; they fixed it for work you did and left it open for work you didn't.

SECTION FOUR · HOW IT LOOKS

It doesn't look like it belongs in this app yet

The fresh pair of eyes measured what the other three only felt. Today, Bank and Record all share one discipline: one thing is lit, everything else is grey, and a domain colour is a thin rule on the edge of a neutral card. Goals paints that palette around seven full-bleed cards — it measured eight accent-outlined elements, seven of them identical, repeating the same kicker verbatim. Nothing is lit, so nothing is first.

I checked its three structural claims in the source myself and all three hold:

A1The domain colour is a full outline here — a 3px left rule everywhere else
A2The apex border is gold at 40%; each December card sits at 100% — tier 2 out-shouts tier 1
A2.g2anc .t is the app's only card headline in body font
A3Gold means eight different things on this tab and three on Today
A5The standard section header was re-implemented three times

Its redesign is drawn properly — before and after, four screens, and I've run it through my own harness at 375 / 390 / 440 / 1280 and read the screenshots. The single change it recommends is the right one: light the apex the way Today lights the live card, and quiet the seven December cards to a left rule. One card lit, seven calmed — the page gets a top, and the three tiers become readable with the text blurred out.
