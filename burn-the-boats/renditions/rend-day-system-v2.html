Four agents walked ten days as you — through a new commitment, a collapse, a big win, and slow drift. Two more used it as strangers with no idea who it was built for.

They found fourteen real defects, which are now fixed and live. They also disagreed with four things in the first proposal. This is v1 with their objections applied and your answers locked in.

BUFFER
Dashed empty slots → elastic block lengths. Your point, and all four agreed: air in the day, not holes on the screen.

06 · HOW IT LANDS

And what I'd deliberately not build yet

The two invisible foundations · the daily-page declutter · "See the whole day". Nothing changes how you work. Day 1 opens on a clean surface.

The slot editor and the three run modes — the two you responded to most. By then you'll have a week of real use telling me where I'm wrong.

NOT YET
The planner comes after you've lived a week — deliberately. A planning ritual designed for a week I've only imagined is exactly the thing the research says gets abandoned by Wednesday.
The one thing worth knowing. Three agents said the app's ethics — never punish absence, never wipe a streak, never fake a number, never render a wall of failure — are better than almost anything shipped. And that the model underneath contradicts them on four screens out of five. Closing that gap is the actual project. The day system is how.
