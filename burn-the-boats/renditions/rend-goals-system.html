Burn the Boats · the goal system · every screen

What it looks like when you put your own goals in.

Twelve screens, drawn accurately against the model in the plan — areas · dates · goals · commitments. Nothing here is hardcoded: every area, every date and every goal on these screens is something you created from the phone.

The content is example content. I've used real-looking businesses so it reads true, but that's you typing — not me deciding. The structure is the proposal; the words in it are placeholders.
