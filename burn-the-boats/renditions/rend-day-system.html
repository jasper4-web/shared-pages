The Day System

Every screen here is a drawing. Nothing is wired, nothing is in the app. This is what we'd be agreeing to before anyone writes a line.

The rule underneath all of it is the one you already gave me: structure bends, the economy doesn't. The four deep blocks stay sacred — twenty a week, always — so capacity stays a constant and the goals keep costing something. What each block is for, when it sits, and what rules police it is yours to change.

ONE THING TO DECIDE BEFORE THE REST

Your day is currently scheduled 06:00 → 21:50 with no gaps. Every study I read named the fully booked day as the main reason people quit time blocking — "abandoned it by Wednesday." The standing advice is to plan 70–80% and leave the rest as buffer. Screen 05 shows what that looks like. It is a real change to how your day feels and it is your call, not mine.

WHAT I STILL NEED FROM YOU

Three answers and I can stop guessing

1 · Buffer. Screen 05, left or right. A fully booked day, or deliberate air in it? Everything I read says the full day is the main reason people quit — but it's your life and you may genuinely run hot.
2 · The phone-allowed block. Same XP as a locked one? I've drawn it that way — same pay, recorded differently. Say if you'd rather it paid less.
3 · One arrangement, or a library? Screen 03 shows a single current shape you pin for N weeks. Do you also want to name and keep arrangements — a "SANO week", a "launch week" — and switch between them? That roughly doubles the surface, so I'd rather know now.
