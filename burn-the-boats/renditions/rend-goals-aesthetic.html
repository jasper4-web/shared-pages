LENS 4 · AESTHETICS · NOTHING BUILT

Goals looks like a
different app. Here's why.

Today, Bank and Record share one discipline: one thing is lit, everything else is grey, and a domain colour is a thin rule at the edge of a neutral card — never the card's outline. Record draws six domains with no colour at all, as hairline rows inside one container.

Goals paints the same palette around seven full-bleed cards. Measured on the live app: eight accent-bordered elements on one screen, seven of them at exactly 364×133px, each with the identical kicker DECEMBER · Dec 25 · 150 days repeated verbatim. Nothing is lit, so nothing is first — and the December goal is the only card headline in the app set in body font (Satoshi 16px), smaller than the capacity line above it.

The fix needs no new components. Move the colour to a 3px left rule, give the apex the one glow, put the December goal in Clash 19px, replace three hand-rolled section headers with h2.sec, and hand the repeated string back to the header's <u> slot.

A1
Colour becomes a rule, not an outline. border-left:3px solid var(--c) on a neutral card — Bank's reward row and Today's .blk .dom, already shipped.
A2
Three tiers, three weights. Clash 27 / Clash 19 / Satoshi 13.5, with left rules at glow · 100% · 28%. Legible with the text blurred out.
A3
Gold means one thing again. It carries eight meanings here and three on Today. The view tabs go violet (Bank's .chip.seg.on); the footer goes ghost.
A4
The apex gets Today's seam — the thing before and after, inside the lit card — which finally gives the WIG somewhere to render (fixes F1-3) and puts the one gold action at the top instead of 2,151px down.
