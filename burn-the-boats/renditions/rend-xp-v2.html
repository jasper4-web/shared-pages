CLEAN · 15 — your vices, every day, all of them. The dishwasher held, the smoke gate kept. All clean = the slice is full. A slip = that vice's share goes quiet for the day. Never red, never a lecture — quiet.

  • The sentence you asked for, as math: perfect everything else + failing your vices every day caps the whole run at ~92. The top rank starts above that line — there is no route to the summit around your vices. Not a punishment; a locked door.

  • "All types of stuff for doing better": clean-streak landmarks (7 · 30 · 60 days) pay real XP through the ledger, and Bank rewards can now carry clean-day terms — "That purchase · 7,000 · AND 14 clean days." Your requirement-gated Bank, pointed at the thing that matters most.
