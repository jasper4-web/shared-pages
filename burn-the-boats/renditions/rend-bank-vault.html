Burn the Boats · Agent 6 · The Vault · final build

The Vault, in seven states.

Every fixture on this screen is a part of the vault: the balance is bullion on a shelf, the privileges are keys on a board, each reward is a numbered deposit box with his own terms engraved on the plate beside it, the boosts are the deposit chute, and the history is the register. Locked, unlocked and emptied are three different physical states of the same box.

Rendered by one function against the shipped data shape — S.xp, S.privileges, S.rewards[], BOOSTS[] — so the seven states below cannot drift from each other.
