FROM 05

Riso Two-Colour

7 refinements

Seven, because this is the one you singled out. Each pushes the blur a different way — number of inks, how far they sit off register, whether the softness is a real blur or a ghost, and whether the screen is flat or gradated. 05·C Riso Halation is the literal answer to “the blurry cool effect”: a genuinely blurred ink laid under a sharp one.

05·A

Riso Three-Ink

Three plates instead of two — a warm red, a leaf green and a cold blue, each screened at its own angle so they moiré against each other. Registration is tight, so the misfit reads as craft. The art stays in full colour; the inks ghost around it.
