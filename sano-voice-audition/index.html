Read this before you judge them

  • The five candidate clips are 2–4 seconds of a generic sample line — not our greeting, and not carrying our settings. You're judging timbre here: does this person sound like someone you'd trust answering your phone. You are not judging delivery.
  • The last two are real recordings off our own line, saying our real greeting. Only her side is in the file — no caller audio, ever. They're already phone audio, so the toggle doesn't change them.
  • Every clip has been levelled to the same loudness so a louder sample can't win by being louder.
  • Once you pick, we render your voice saying our actual greeting before we put it on the line. Nothing changes on the phone until you say so.
  • One catch on Marin. She came from a different engine than the one answering today. Picking her isn't a switch flip — I'd have to prove she works on the current setup first, or move the line back. Pick her anyway if she's the one; I'll go find out.

One setting I want to change at the same time

  • Stability: 0.7 today → 0.40 proposed. Stability is the dullness dial — high keeps her flat and safe, low lets her sound alive. It got pushed up to 0.7 to cover stumbles from the old worn-out voice. A current voice doesn't stumble, and phone audio flattens everything anyway, so 0.7 on the phone is just dull. Dull is the one failure state we wrote down.
